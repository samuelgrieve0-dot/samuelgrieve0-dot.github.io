Folklore Studio

Folklore Studio is the private publishing tool used by the Folklore Sports channel. It uploads and schedules the channel's own videos, manages their titles, thumbnails, captions and comments, reads the channel's own analytics, and saves the channel's own files to Google Drive. It is used only by the channel owner.
